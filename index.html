Interpretation boundary

Association is not causation

SHAP decomposes model predictions. It identifies influential predictors and spatial heterogeneity but does not establish causal effects.

Methods and meaning

Methods & Notes

A transparent guide to the analytical sequence, definitions, and limits behind every view.

Source manifest
01
Calibrate population

Five-minute mobility observations are time-corrected and calibrated to official 2018 population totals.

02
Match PM₂.₅

Hourly population and pollution are combined on a national 10 km grid to estimate exposure.

03
Contrast periods

Daily values are averaged into pre-festival, festival, and post-festival periods.

04
Explain change

Decomposition, Moran/LISA, machine learning, spatial CV, and SHAP reveal distinct dimensions of the pattern.

P Population

Mobility component

C₀ × (P₁ − P₀)

Change associated with the redistribution of population activity while baseline pollution is held constant.

C Concentration

Pollution component

P₀ × (C₁ − C₀)

Change associated with PM₂.₅ variation while baseline population is held constant.

P×C Joint movement

Interaction component

(P₁ − P₀) × (C₁ − C₀)

The residual joint effect when mobility and pollution change at the same time.

Moran’s I and LISA

Global Moran’s I

Measures whether similar change values cluster across neighbouring 10 km cells.

High–High / Low–Low

Local clusters where a cell and its neighbours share consistently high or low values.

High–Low / Low–High

Spatial outliers whose values contrast with their local neighbourhood.

Data and interpretation notes

  • 01 February contains 16 observed hours and is retained as incomplete rather than imputed.
  • Blank SHAP cells fall outside complete model support; they are not zero contributions.
  • Prediction diagnostics use sampled held-out test support and are not national estimates.
  • The validated boundary workflow explicitly assigns EPSG:4326 where source metadata is absent.
  • Model fit and SHAP values describe predictive associations, not causal effects.

Evaluation metrics

How model fit is read

R²

Explained variance; negative spatial values are worse than a fold-specific mean baseline.

RMSE

Root mean squared error, with greater sensitivity to large residuals.

MAE

Mean absolute error, a more robust summary of typical error magnitude.

Research limitations

Boundaries of inference

  • Platform counts proxy population activity; they are not direct observations of migration or resident population.
  • Models and SHAP use complete cases, so their support is smaller than the full descriptive grid.
  • Decomposition components are mathematical constructions based on the exposure identity.
  • Model performance and SHAP explain prediction, not causal mechanisms.
  • Extreme urban grids can strongly influence national totals and error distributions.
Reproducible static release

Published values are aggregated research outputs generated from the formal analysis. No raw platform records or direct personal identifiers are included. The source manifest records relative paths, byte sizes, and SHA-256 hashes for provenance.
